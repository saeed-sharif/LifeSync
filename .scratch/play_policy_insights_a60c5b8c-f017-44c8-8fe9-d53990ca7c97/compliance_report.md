### Permissions

#### The app requests the restricted 'android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM' permission in AndroidManifest.xml, but as a productivity task and routine planner, it does not qualify for this permission under Google Play policy.
- **Policy**: **Exact Alarm Policy**
- **Severity**: 🟡 Important
- **Files**: [AndroidManifest.xml](file://app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml), [AlarmScheduler.kt](file://app/src/main/java/com/dalgeer/lifeplanner/util/AlarmScheduler.kt), [PermissionUtils.kt](file://app/src/main/java/com/dalgeer/lifeplanner/util/PermissionUtils.kt)
- **Evidence**: app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml line 6 declares <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM" /> alongside SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM. The app is a task/routine planner (com.dalgeer.lifeplanner) using exact alarms for task and routine reminders in AlarmScheduler.kt.

**Critic Verification**: AndroidManifest.xml line 6 declares android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM. Under Google Play Policy, USE_EXACT_ALARM is restricted to apps whose primary core function is an alarm clock, timer, or calendar app with event reminders. As a task and routine planner, LifePlanner does not qualify for USE_EXACT_ALARM and must rely exclusively on SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM. (Confidence: High)
- **Recommendation**: Remove <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM" /> from app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml. Rely instead on android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM with runtime checks using PermissionUtils.canScheduleExactAlarms().
- **References**:
  - https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16558241#exact_alarm
